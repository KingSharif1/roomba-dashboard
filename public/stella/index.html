1. Overview

Stella is a Roomba 694 turned into a home robot. The Roomba still drives, docks and cleans. A clear deck on top carries a computer, a lidar, a camera and a voice.

She talks to the Roomba over its 7-pin serial port (iRobot Open Interface) and runs on the Roomba's own battery.

Base
Roomba 694
Li-ion 14.4 V (16.8 V full)
OI serial · 115200 baud

2.8 Power chain

battery + → 3 A fuse → XH-M609 cutoff (12.8 V off / 13.4 V on) → rocker → DROK 5 A buck @ 5.15 V → USB-C → Pi 4 → USB → Mega · lidar · mic

Power is tapped straight from the battery. The data cable's power pins sit behind a ~200 mA fuse inside the Roomba; Pi + lidar need ~1 A.

Rule
Never power the Pi from a power bank or laptop USB.

5. Build log

  1. SEP · problemThe original Wi-Fi main board's firmware blocked the serial port. Power and ground fine, serial pins at 0 V.
  2. 2026-09-23 · fixOrdered a used 600-series main board (~$22).
  3. 2026-09-29 · breakthroughReplacement board + correct 7-pin cable: the Roomba talks. Green wire identified as BRC (grounding it drops the baud to 19200). ROS 2 driver drives the Roomba and publishes odometry.
  4. 2026-09-29 · lidarRPLIDAR C1 running in ROS 2, health OK.
  5. 2026-10-11 · problemThe Pi 4 died after running on laptop USB and a power bank: under-voltage and throttling at 85 °C.
  6. 2026-10-11 · fixReplacement Pi 4 ordered. Power redesigned: battery tap → fuse → cutoff → rocker → 5 A buck at 5.15 V.
  7. 2026-10-11 · deck10 in plate, lidar centered on 35 mm posts, Pi at the back, speaker + mic ordered.
  8. nextRebuild on the new Pi → mount lidar → first map → voice.
